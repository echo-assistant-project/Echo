# Study and exam preparation

## 1. Know the target
Download the official syllabus and exam pattern; collect previous years' papers. Mark topics by weightage and your current strength (strong / okay / weak).

## 2. The plan
Weeks until the exam → phase 1 (60%) learn and make notes, phase 2 (25%) revision and practice, phase 3 (15%) full mock tests and weak areas. Each day: 2–3 subjects, hardest when fresh, time-boxed sessions (50 minutes study, 10 break), one revision slot.

## 3. Methods that actually work
- **Active recall**: close the book and write/say what you remember; flashcards; solve questions before re-reading.
- **Spaced revision**: revisit topics after 1, 3, 7, 21 days (Echo can remind you).
- **Practice questions** daily — they show what the exam really asks.
- **Teach it**: explain the topic aloud to someone (or Echo) in simple words.
Highlighting and re-reading feel productive but help least.

## 4. Notes
Short, in your words, one page per topic: key ideas, formulas, diagrams, common mistakes. For competitive exams, keep a "mistakes notebook" from every mock test.

## 5. Mock tests
Full timed mocks weekly in the last phase, under exam conditions. Analyse each: questions wrong because of concept, careless error, or time — fix the cause, not just the question.

## 6. Exam day
Sleep well the two nights before, light breakfast, reach early, read instructions, attempt easy questions first, manage time per section, negative marking: skip pure guesses.

## 7. Health and stress
Exercise and sleep improve memory; short breaks beat all-nighters. If anxiety is heavy, see stress-and-wellbeing; Tele MANAS (14416) is free 24×7.
