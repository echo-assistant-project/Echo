# Sales basics

## 1. Selling is helping someone decide
Your job is to understand the buyer's problem and show — honestly — whether your product solves it. Pushing a bad fit costs you referrals.

## 2. Leads
Keep a simple list (sheet or CRM): name, source, need, budget, timeline, next step and date. Every lead always has a next step.

## 3. First contact
Personal, short, about them: "Hi Ravi, you asked about 2BHK flats near the secretariat. I have three ready-to-move options under ₹60 lakh. Can I share details on WhatsApp?" Respond to enquiries within an hour — speed wins deals.

## 4. Discovery questions (listen 70%, talk 30%)
What made you look now? What have you tried? What matters most (price, location, timeline)? Who else decides? What's your budget range? What would make this a great decision for you? Repeat back what you heard.

## 5. Present value, not features
Link each feature to their stated need: "You said your parents will live with you — this ground-floor unit means no stairs." Use proof: photos, testimonials, numbers.

## 6. Objections
Acknowledge → ask → answer. "Too expensive": "I understand. Compared to what?" then show value, payment options, or a smaller option. "I'll think about it": "Of course — what's the one thing you're unsure about?" Never pressure with false urgency.

## 7. Close and follow up
Ask clearly: "Shall I book the site visit for Saturday 11 am?" If not now, agree the next date. Follow up 3–5 times, each with something useful (new photos, a price update), then politely close the loop. After the sale: thank, deliver well, ask for a review and referrals.
