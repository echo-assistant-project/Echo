# Income tax (India)

Rules change every Budget — confirm figures on incometax.gov.in for the year you're filing. Use a CA for business income, capital gains or notices.

## 1. Key words
**FY** (the year you earn, Apr–Mar) and **AY** (the next year, when you file). **PAN** linked with Aadhaar. **TDS**: tax already cut by employer/bank. **Form 16**: employer's TDS certificate. **AIS / Form 26AS**: the department's record of your income and TDS — always check it before filing.

## 2. New regime (default) — FY 2025-26 as announced
Basic exemption ₹4 lakh, slabs rising in steps; standard deduction ₹75,000 for salaried and pensioners; Section 87A rebate makes tax nil for resident individuals with taxable income up to ₹12 lakh (so salary up to about ₹12.75 lakh). Most deductions (80C, HRA) aren't available in the new regime. The rebate doesn't cover special-rate capital gains.

## 3. Old regime
Lower exemption but deductions: 80C (₹1.5 lakh: PPF, EPF, ELSS, life insurance, principal of home loan, tuition), 80D (health insurance), HRA, home-loan interest (Section 24, up to ₹2 lakh for self-occupied), 80CCD(1B) NPS. Compare both regimes with the official calculator; salaried people can choose each year.

## 4. Which ITR form (typical)
ITR-1: salary/pension, one house, other income up to limits. ITR-2: capital gains, more than one house. ITR-3: business/profession. ITR-4: presumptive business income.

## 5. Filing steps
Log in to incometax.gov.in → check AIS/26AS → File Income Tax Return → choose AY and form → the pre-filled data appears → verify salary, interest, deductions → pay any balance tax (challan) → submit → **e-verify within 30 days** (Aadhaar OTP is easiest). Deadline for most individuals: 31 July (check the year's date).

## 6. Refunds and notices
Refund comes to the pre-validated bank account; track it on the portal. A notice: read the section and deadline, log in to the portal to see it (fake notices arrive by email/SMS with links), respond in time; get a CA for anything beyond simple mismatch corrections.

## 7. Saving tax honestly
Choose the better regime, invest in eligible options only if they fit your goals, keep proofs, and report all income (interest, rent, capital gains) — the AIS already knows.
