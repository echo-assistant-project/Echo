# Consumer complaints (India)

General information, not legal advice; for large claims, consult a lawyer.

## 1. Gather evidence first
Invoice/order ID, warranty, photos/videos of the defect, chat and email screenshots, call dates, delivery receipts, payment proof.

## 2. Complain to the company in writing
Email/customer portal: what you bought (date, order number), what went wrong, what you want (repair, replacement, refund, compensation), and a deadline (7–15 days). Keep the complaint/ticket number. Escalate to the grievance officer (e-commerce sites must publish one).

## 3. National Consumer Helpline
Call **1915** or register on consumerhelpline.gov.in / NCH app / WhatsApp. They forward it to the company and track it — many cases settle here without any court.

## 4. Sector regulators (often faster)
Banks and UPI: bank's grievance cell, then **RBI Ombudsman** via cms.rbi.org.in. Insurance: Bima Bharosa / Insurance Ombudsman. Telecom: TRAI-mandated appellate authority of the operator. Stock brokers/funds: SEBI SCORES. Property projects: state **RERA** (rera.ap.gov.in in AP) for delayed possession or false promises.

## 5. Consumer Commission (Consumer Protection Act, 2019)
File online on **e-Daakhil**: District Commission for claims up to ₹50 lakh, State up to ₹2 crore, National above (check current limits). Within 2 years of the problem. You can file yourself, without a lawyer; small fees for higher amounts. Send a legal-notice-style letter first; it strengthens the case.

## 6. Your rights in short
Safety, information, choice, being heard, redress, and consumer education. Unfair trade practices include misleading ads, hidden charges, and refusing to give a bill. An MRP-plus charge, forcing "no refund", or not honouring warranty can be challenged.

## 7. Template line
"I bought [product] on [date] (order [no.]) for ₹[amount]. It [problem] on [date]. Despite complaints on [dates] (ticket [no.]), it isn't resolved. I request [refund/replacement] within 15 days, failing which I'll approach the Consumer Commission."
