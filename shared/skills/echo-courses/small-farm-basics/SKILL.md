# Small farm basics (India)

For crop-specific advice, the local **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)**, agriculture officer, or **Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551)** knows local soils and weather best.

## 1. Know the soil
Get a soil test (Soil Health Card scheme via the agriculture office/KVK): pH, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, organic carbon. Fertilise by the card's recommendation — over-use of urea wastes money and harms soil. Add farmyard manure/compost and green manure crops.

## 2. Choose crops
Match season (kharif: monsoon; rabi: winter; zaid: summer), water available, soil, local market demand and price trends, and risk. Mix crops or intercrop to spread risk; include pulses to improve soil. Buy certified seed from authorised dealers and keep the bill.

## 3. Water
Drip and sprinkler irrigation save 30–50% water and often raise yields; subsidies are available under PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (micro-irrigation) — ask the horticulture/agriculture department. Farm ponds store monsoon water. Irrigate in the morning or evening.

## 4. Pests: integrated pest management (IPM)
Scout fields weekly, identify the pest correctly (photo to KVK), use resistant varieties, crop rotation, pheromone/yellow sticky traps, neem-based sprays, and chemical pesticides only when damage crosses the threshold — the right product and dose, with protective clothing, never near children or water sources.

## 5. Government support (check eligibility on official portals)
**PM-KISAN** income support (check status on pmkisan.gov.in with Aadhaar/mobile), **PMFBY** crop insurance (enrol before the cut-off date for the season), **Kisan Credit Card** for low-interest crop loans, **Soil Health Card**, state schemes (in AP, check the Rythu Seva Kendras/RBKs and state portals).

## 6. Selling better
Check prices on **eNAM** and local mandis before selling, grade and clean produce, store safely, join or form a Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO) for better prices and inputs.

## 7. Keep records
Seeds, fertiliser, labour, water, yields and prices per field per season — it shows which crops actually make money and helps with loans and insurance claims.
