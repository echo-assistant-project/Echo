# Cooking basics

Echo can scale recipes, convert cups to grams, and set timers ("timer 12 minutes for rice").

## 1. A starter kitchen
Pressure cooker, kadai, one non-stick pan, saucepan, sharp knife, chopping board, mixer-grinder. Pantry: rice, atta, toor/moong dal, oil, ghee, mustard and cumin seeds, turmeric, red chilli, coriander powder, garam masala, salt, onions, tomatoes, ginger-garlic, green chillies.

## 2. Core techniques
- **Tadka/tempering**: heat oil, add mustard/cumin until they crackle, then curry leaves, garlic, dry chillies — pour over dal or start a curry.
- **Onion-tomato base (masala)**: onions until golden, ginger-garlic 1 minute, powdered spices on low for 30 seconds (add a splash of water so they don't burn), tomatoes until oil separates. This base makes most curries.
- **Pressure cooking**: dal 3–4 whistles, soaked rajma/chana 5–6 whistles plus 10 minutes on low; let pressure release naturally.
- **Rice**: rinse, 1 cup rice to 2 cups water (less for basmati soaked 20 minutes), cook covered on low.

## 3. Five dishes to learn first
Dal tadka, jeera rice, egg or paneer bhurji, a mixed vegetable sabzi, curd rice/upma. Then rajma/chana and one chicken or fish curry.

## 4. Fixing mistakes
Too salty: add plain boiled potato pieces or more unsalted base/curd. Too spicy: add curd, cream, coconut milk or a pinch of sugar. Too thin: simmer uncovered. Burnt bottom: don't stir — transfer the top to a new pan.

## 5. Food safety (WHO five keys)
Keep clean (wash hands, boards), separate raw meat from cooked food, cook thoroughly (chicken until juices run clear), keep food at safe temperatures (fridge within 2 hours, especially in Indian heat), use safe water and fresh ingredients.

## 6. Leftovers
Cool quickly, refrigerate in closed containers, eat within 1–2 days, reheat until steaming hot, reheat rice only once.

## 7. Cook smarter
Prep twice a week (chopped onions, ginger-garlic paste, boiled dal), cook once eat twice, and plan meals with the lazy-meal-plan skill.
