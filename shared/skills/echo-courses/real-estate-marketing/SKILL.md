# Real estate marketing

## 1. The listing that sells
**Headline**: type, size, place, one strong point ("Ready 2BHK, 1,150 sq ft, 5 min from Amaravati Secretariat").
**Facts first**: price (or "from ₹…"), carpet area, floor, facing, parking, possession date, RERA number.
**Benefits next**: what living there is like — schools, hospital, road access, water source, quiet street.
**Call to action**: "WhatsApp for the brochure and a site visit slot."

## 2. Photos and video
Daylight, lights on, wide-angle but honest, clean rooms, straight verticals. Order: exterior → living → kitchen → bedrooms → bathrooms → view → amenities → location map. A 60-second walkthrough video and a drone shot for plots outperform text.

## 3. Pricing
Compare 5–10 recent comparable sales (same area, size, age), adjust for floor, facing and amenities, and check the government market value on IGRS. Overpricing makes listings go stale; price at the market and let demand set the final number.

## 4. Where to list
Your website (each property its own page, fast, with map and enquiry form), the big portals, Google Business Profile posts, local Facebook groups, and broadcast lists of people who opted in. Keep one master sheet so price changes update everywhere.

## 5. Lead handling
Reply within minutes, qualify (budget, timeline, loan or cash, who decides), send the brochure, book a site visit with a reminder the day before, follow up after with a summary and next step. Log every lead with its source.

## 6. Site visits
Prepare documents (approvals, RERA, EC), a printed price sheet, answers about maintenance and water, and refreshments. Show the best room last. Note objections and answer them in the follow-up.

## 7. Rules and trust
Show the RERA registration number of the project (and agent) in ads; never promise returns or guaranteed appreciation; no misleading photos or "approved" claims without approval; get consent before marketing messages.
