# Investing basics (India)

Education, not advice. For personal recommendations use a SEBI-registered investment adviser (RIA).

## 1. Before investing
Emergency fund ready, high-interest debt cleared, health and term insurance in place, goals written with dates.

## 2. Risk and return
Higher expected return comes with higher ups and downs. Inflation (~5–6%) quietly eats money kept idle. Time in the market reduces the effect of short-term swings; money needed within 3 years should not be in equity.

## 3. The main options
- **Fixed deposits / RD**: safe, fixed return, interest taxed at your slab.
- **PPF**: government-backed, 15-year lock-in, tax-free interest (EEE), good for long safe savings.
- **NPS**: retirement, low cost, part locked until 60.
- **Mutual funds**: pooled money run by professionals. **Index funds/ETFs** copy an index (Nifty 50) at low cost — a strong default for long-term equity. Debt funds for medium-term, lower risk.
- **Direct stocks**: only with time to research; keep it a small part at first.
- **Gold**: 5–10% as a hedge; Sovereign Gold Bonds (when issued) or gold ETFs rather than jewellery.
- **Real estate**: see property-investment-math.

## 4. SIP and asset allocation
A SIP invests a fixed amount monthly, averaging the buying price. Choose an allocation by goal and age (e.g. long goal: 60–70% equity index funds, rest debt/PPF), rebalance yearly. Choose **direct plans** (lower expense ratio) and check the expense ratio and fund's long-term record — not last year's return.

## 5. Taxes (check current rules)
Equity gains held over 1 year are long-term (taxed above an exemption limit); under 1 year short-term at a higher rate. Debt fund gains and FD interest are mostly taxed at your slab.

## 6. Scam and mis-selling alarms
"Guaranteed" high returns, Telegram/WhatsApp tip groups, unregistered "advisers", pressure to act today, insurance sold as investment with low returns, crypto schemes promising fixed income. Check registrations on SEBI's website; complain on SEBI SCORES.

## 7. A simple starter plan
Open a demat/mutual fund account with KYC, start a small monthly SIP in a Nifty 50 index fund, add PPF for safe savings, increase the SIP when income rises, review once a year — and don't check prices daily.
