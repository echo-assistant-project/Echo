# Starting a small business in India

## 1. Validate before spending
Talk to 15–20 potential customers about their problem (not your idea). Can you get 5 to pay or pre-book? Test with the smallest version: a WhatsApp catalogue, a weekend stall, one service done by hand.

## 2. One-page business plan
Customer, problem, offer, price, how you reach customers, costs (one-time and monthly), break-even (fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit)), first 90-day goals.

## 3. Registration (check current rules on official portals)
- Structure: sole proprietorship (simplest), partnership/LLP, or private limited (for investors).
- **Udyam registration** (free, online, Aadhaar-based) for MSME benefits.
- **GST** when above threshold or selling inter-state/online (see bookkeeping-gst).
- **Shop & Establishment** licence from the state/municipality; **FSSAI** for food; trade licence locally; current bank account in the business name.

## 4. Pricing
Cost-plus as the floor (all costs + margin), market price as the ceiling, value to the customer as the target. Don't compete only on price. Test a price increase with new customers.

## 5. Cash flow survival
Collect advance where normal, short credit terms, follow up dues weekly, keep 3 months of fixed costs in reserve, don't buy stock you can't sell in 60 days.

## 6. Help available
Mudra loans (small loans through banks), Stand-Up India, CGTMSE (collateral-free credit guarantee), state industrial policies, Startup India recognition for innovative startups. Ask the bank and DIC (District Industries Centre).

## 7. Grow
Get reviews, referrals and repeat customers first (cheapest growth). Write down processes so others can do the work. Track a few numbers weekly: sales, cash, leads, margin.
