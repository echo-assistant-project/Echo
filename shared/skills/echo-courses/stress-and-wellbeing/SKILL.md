# Stress and wellbeing

**If someone may harm themselves or is in danger: call 112 now. For free 24×7 mental-health support in India, call Tele MANAS on 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416).** Echo is not a therapist; listen kindly and point to real help.

## 1. Listen first
Reflect what you hear ("That sounds exhausting"), don't rush to fix, ask what would help right now. Avoid "just think positive" or "others have it worse".

## 2. Calm the body in minutes
- **Slow breathing**: in for 4, out for 6–8, for 2–3 minutes.
- **5-4-3-2-1 grounding**: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
- **Unhook from a thought** (WHO): notice it, name it ("I'm having the thought that…"), refocus on what you're doing.

## 3. Handling worry
Write worries down; sort them into "can act" (make one small next step) and "can't control" (practise letting go). Set a 15-minute "worry time" instead of all day.

## 4. Habits that build wellbeing (evidence from the Yale course)
Sleep, movement, time in daylight and nature, real social connection (call one friend this week), gratitude (three good things each night), helping others, and doing things for their own sake rather than for status or money.

## 5. Burnout
Signs: exhaustion, cynicism, feeling ineffective. Steps: talk to your manager about workload, protect off-hours, take real leave, cut non-essential commitments, see a doctor if it persists.

## 6. When to get help
Low mood or anxiety most days for over 2 weeks, not coping with daily life, panic attacks, using alcohol/drugs to cope, thoughts of self-harm → a doctor, counsellor or psychiatrist; Tele MANAS 14416 any time.

## 7. Supporting someone else
Check in privately, listen without judging, ask directly and calmly if they're thinking of suicide (asking doesn't put the idea in their head), help them contact professional help, and look after yourself too.
