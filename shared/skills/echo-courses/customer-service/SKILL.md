# Customer service

## 1. Speed and clarity
Reply within an hour during working hours, even if only to say "Got it — I'll check and update you by 4 pm." Then keep that promise. Every reply ends with what happens next.

## 2. Handling a complaint (the HEARD method)
**H**ear them out without interrupting · **E**mpathise ("I understand how frustrating it is to wait a week") · **A**pologise for the experience, sincerely · **R**esolve: offer a concrete fix and a time · **D**iagnose afterwards why it happened so it doesn't repeat.

## 3. Angry customers
Stay calm and slow; don't take it personally; use their name; focus on what you *can* do. If they're abusive, set a boundary politely and offer to continue later or in writing.

## 4. Replying to reviews
- **Bad review**: thank, apologise, one line of fact without arguing, invite them to a private channel with a name and number, fix it, and ask if they'd update the review.
- **Good review**: thank them by name and mention something specific.
- Never post fake reviews or offer money for good ones.

## 5. Refunds and returns
Clear written policy (what, how long, condition), quick process, and say exactly when the money will reach them (UPI/card timelines).

## 6. Systems for a small business
Saved replies for common questions, a simple log of complaints (date, issue, fix, time taken), a weekly 10-minute review of patterns, and asking happy customers for reviews.
