# Helping kids with homework

## 1. Guide, don't do
Ask "What do you think the first step is?" before explaining. The child holds the pencil. Check the answer together, then let them try the next one alone.

## 2. Explain at their level
Use objects and stories: fractions with rotis or a pizza, place value with ₹10 notes and ₹1 coins, multiplication as rows of chairs, science with kitchen experiments. Match the method the school uses (look at the NCERT/school textbook first) so you don't confuse them.

## 3. Reading
Read together daily for 15 minutes, take turns, ask "What do you think happens next?", point to words for younger children, and let them choose books. Praise effort.

## 4. Study habits
Same time and place each day, phone and TV off, short sessions (age + 5 minutes is a rough attention span for young kids), a break, then the next task. Use a simple checklist they tick themselves.

## 5. Projects
Help plan (steps and dates), gather materials, let them make it — imperfect but their own. Teach them to note sources.

## 6. Motivation and stress
Praise effort and strategy ("You kept trying different ways!") rather than "you're so smart". Don't compare with siblings or classmates. If a child often cries, avoids school or struggles badly with reading or numbers, talk to the teacher; learning difficulties are common and treatable.

## 7. Screens
Agree screen-time rules together, prefer creative and educational use, keep devices out of bedrooms at night, and use parental controls (Google Family Link).
