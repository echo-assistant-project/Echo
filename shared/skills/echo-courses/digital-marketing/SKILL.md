# Digital marketing

## 1. Know who and why
Write: the customer (who, where, what problem), the offer (what you sell, price, why you), and the one action you want (call, WhatsApp, visit, book). Everything else serves that action.

## 2. Free foundations first
- **Google Business Profile**: correct name, category, hours, phone, photos, and asking happy customers for reviews (reply to every review). This is the biggest win for local businesses.
- **A simple website/landing page**: what you offer, prices or "starting from", photos, location map, a click-to-call and WhatsApp button, fast on mobile.
- **WhatsApp Business**: catalogue, quick replies, labels, broadcast lists only to people who opted in.

## 3. SEO (being found on Google)
Pages that answer what people search ("2BHK flats for rent in Amaravati"), a clear title and description per page, headings, real photos with alt text, fast loading, links from local directories and partners. Results take weeks to months — be patient and consistent.

## 4. Social media
Pick one or two platforms where your customers are. Post a mix: useful tips, behind the scenes, customer stories, offers (no more than 1 in 5). Short vertical videos reach the most people. Consistency beats volume: 3 good posts a week.

## 5. Paid ads on a small budget
Start with ₹300–500/day, one goal, one audience (location radius + interests), 2–3 ad versions, a clear call to action. Run 7 days, keep the winner, stop the rest. Track cost per lead, not likes.

## 6. Measure
Google Analytics / Search Console for the website; ask every new customer "how did you hear about us?" and log it. Monthly: leads by channel, cost per lead, conversion to sale. Do more of what works.

## 7. Rules
Honest claims (no fake reviews — they break platform rules and the law), get consent before messaging, follow RERA rules for property ads (show the RERA number).
