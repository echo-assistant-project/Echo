# Renting a home (India)

General information; rent laws vary by state — check yours, and use a lawyer for disputes.

## 1. Before you agree (tenant)
Visit twice (day and evening), check water supply and pressure, power backup, phone signal, leaks/damp, parking, society rules (pets, bachelors), and the landlord's ownership (property tax receipt or sale deed copy).

## 2. The agreement — must include
Names and IDs, address, rent and due date, deposit (amount and refund timeline), maintenance and who pays which bills, lock-in period, notice period (usually 1–2 months), annual increase (e.g. 5%), who repairs what, furniture/fixtures list, permitted use, and signatures of both plus two witnesses.

## 3. 11-month vs registered lease
Many use 11-month agreements on stamp paper to avoid compulsory registration (required for leases over 12 months). A registered lease gives stronger proof. Pay the correct stamp duty (state rates; AP details on IGRS). The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 suggests caps like 2 months' deposit for homes, where states have adopted it.

## 4. Police verification
Most cities require tenant verification — the landlord submits it (online on many state police portals). Do it; it protects both sides.

## 5. Move-in checklist
Photos/video of every room with date, meter readings, keys count, inventory signed by both. Pay rent by bank/UPI for a record, and ask for rent receipts (needed for HRA).

## 6. During the stay
Repairs: structural and major items usually the landlord's, small wear-and-tear the tenant's — follow the agreement. Raise issues in writing (WhatsApp/email). The landlord must give notice before visits.

## 7. Moving out
Give notice in writing as per the agreement, repaint/clean only if agreed, hand over keys against a signed note, compare with move-in photos, and claim the deposit (minus documented dues) within the agreed time. Disputes: polite written demand → legal notice → rent authority/civil court or consumer forum as applicable.
