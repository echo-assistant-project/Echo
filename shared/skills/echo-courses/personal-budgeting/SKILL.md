# Personal budgeting

Echo can track it: "add expense 450 groceries", "set budget 30000", "my expenses this month".

## 1. Know where it goes (2 weeks)
Record every spend — UPI history and bank statements make it easy. Group into: home (rent/EMI, bills), food, transport, family, health, education, fun, other.

## 2. A simple rule to start
**50/30/20** of take-home pay: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% saving and debt repayment. Adjust for Indian realities (family support, EMIs), but keep saving at least 10–20%.

## 3. Pay yourself first
On salary day, move savings automatically: SIP, RD, or a separate savings account. Spend what's left, not save what's left.

## 4. Emergency fund
3–6 months of essential expenses in a liquid, safe place (savings account, FD with no penalty, liquid fund). Build it before investing in anything risky.

## 5. Debt, in order
Stop adding new credit card debt (interest ~36–42% a year). Pay minimum on all, then extra on the highest-interest debt first (avalanche), or the smallest balance first if motivation matters (snowball). Avoid instant-loan apps; check the lender is RBI-registered.

## 6. Cutting costs painlessly
Cancel unused subscriptions, review mobile/internet plans, cook more, buy groceries with a list, wait 48 hours before big wants, compare insurance renewals, use public transport/carpool when easy.

## 7. Goals
Name each goal, amount and date (laptop ₹60,000 in 10 months = ₹6,000/month). Short goals (<3 years) in safe options; long goals can use equity mutual funds (see investing-basics-india).

## 8. Monthly 15-minute review
Planned vs actual by category, one change for next month, celebrate progress. Involve the family so everyone pulls the same way.
