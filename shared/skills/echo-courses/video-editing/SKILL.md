# Video editing

## 1. Plan in 5 lines
Goal, audience, hook (first 3 seconds), 3 key moments, call to action. Short videos: 15–60 seconds. Write the shot list before filming.

## 2. Shooting on a phone
Clean lens, steady (tripod or two hands), good light facing the subject, clear audio (a ₹500 clip mic beats any camera upgrade), shoot in the final orientation (vertical for Reels/Shorts, horizontal for YouTube), extra B-roll of details, 1080p at 30 fps is plenty.

## 3. Editing workflow
1. Import and **rough cut**: keep only the best takes, cut pauses and "umm"s.
2. **Story order**: hook → content → payoff → call to action.
3. **B-roll** over talking parts; cut on action; keep shots 2–5 seconds for short-form.
4. **Audio**: voice clear, music 20–30% under voice, use royalty-free music (YouTube Audio Library, CapCut/Clipchamp libraries).
5. **Text and captions**: most people watch muted — add auto-captions and check them.
6. **Colour**: small brightness/contrast/warmth fix; keep it natural.
7. Export: 1080×1920 (vertical) or 1920×1080, H.264, high bitrate.

## 4. Which editor
Phone: CapCut, InShot, Instagram/YouTube built-ins. Windows: **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11, easy), **DaVinci Resolve** (free, professional). Learn shortcuts for split, ripple delete and zoom.

## 5. Publishing
Title with the keyword people search, thumbnail with a clear face/object and 3–4 big words, description with links and timestamps, post consistently (same days), reply to comments in the first hour.

## 6. Property walkthroughs
Start outside, walk the natural route slowly, stabilised (gimbal or slow steps), lights on, add captions for room sizes and features, end with location highlights and contact details.

## 7. Rules
Use only music and clips you have rights to, get consent from people filmed, and keep claims honest.
