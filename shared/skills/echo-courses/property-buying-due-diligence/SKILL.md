# Buying property safely (India, with AP specifics)

Echo explains the checks; for any purchase, a property lawyer should verify the documents before money changes hands.

## 1. Title: does the seller really own it?
- **Sale deed / title deed** and the **link documents** (chain of ownership) for at least 30 years.
- Seller's ID matches the name on records; for inherited property, legal heir certificate and all heirs' consent; for power-of-attorney sales, be extra careful.

## 2. Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
Shows registered transactions (sales, mortgages) on the property. In AP, search and download on IGRS AP (registration.ap.gov.in) by document number or survey number; check 30+ years. An unreleased mortgage = the property still secures someone's loan.

## 3. Land records (plots and agricultural land)
In AP, Meebhoomi shows **1-B (Record of Rights)**, **Adangal (Pahani)**, village maps and mutation status. The owner's name and extent must match the deed. Agricultural land needs conversion (NALA) before building. In Amaravati, check whether the plot is a CRDA/APCRDA returnable plot (LPS) and its current allotment papers.

## 4. Approvals
- Layout approval (APCRDA/DTCP/municipality) and building plan approval matching what's built.
- **RERA registration** for projects with more than 8 units or over 500 sq m: verify on rera.ap.gov.in — completion date, approved plans, carpet area, complaints.
- Occupancy/completion certificate for ready flats; property tax receipts; no-dues for electricity and water.

## 5. Builder and site checks
Past projects delivered on time, visits to the site at different times, actual carpet area vs "super built-up", road access, flood history (ask neighbours in monsoon), distance to essentials.

## 6. Costs beyond the price (check current rates on IGRS)
Stamp duty + registration fee (state rates, on the higher of sale value and market value), GST on under-construction flats, legal fees, loan processing, maintenance deposit, interiors.

## 7. Registration and after
Agreement of sale with payment schedule and penalties → home loan sanction (bank also checks title) → registration at the Sub-Registrar office with both parties, witnesses, IDs → mutation in revenue/municipal records in your name → update property tax and utilities.

## 8. Red flags — stop and get a lawyer
Price far below market, cash-only demands, seller in a hurry, unclear survey numbers, government or assigned land, disputes in court, no RERA number for a large project, "just pay token today".
