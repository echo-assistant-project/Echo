# Quick trip plan

Ask only what matters: **where (or "suggest"), how many days, who's going (kids? elderly?), rough budget**. Assume sensible defaults for the rest and say what you assumed.

## The plan
- **Day by day**, at most 2–3 things a day, with rest time. With elderly people or small children: less walking, a midday break, nothing too early.
- For each day: morning / afternoon / evening, travel time between places, and one food suggestion.
- **Budget** in ₹ as a small table: travel, stay, food, local transport, tickets, buffer (10%).

## Book in this order
1. Train/flight/bus (prices rise first), 2. stay, 3. anything with limited tickets. Echo can open the booking sites or check the weather for the dates.

## Packing list
- Documents (ID, tickets, booking confirmations offline), medicines (with the **medicine-routine** list), chargers and power bank, weather-specific clothes, a small first-aid kit.

## Keep it honest
- Prices and timings change: say "check current prices" and never present guesses as bookings.
- Offer to add reminders: booking deadlines, the day before departure ("pack tonight"), check-out time.
