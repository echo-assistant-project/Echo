# Lazy meal plan

Ask at most **two** questions: what's at home (or "nothing"), and any no-go foods (vegetarian, allergies). Then decide for them — don't offer endless options.

## The plan
- 7 dinners (and lunches only if asked). Each one: name, **under 30 minutes**, **one pot or pan** where possible, 5–7 ingredients.
- Reuse ingredients across the week so nothing is wasted: one bunch of coriander, one pack of paneer, one tray of eggs.
- Two "zero-effort" nights: leftovers, or something like curd rice / egg bhurji / instant upma.
- One "cook double" night whose leftovers become next day's lunch.

Format:

| Day | Meal | Time | Uses up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Tomato rice + boiled eggs | 25 min | tomatoes, eggs |

## One shopping list
- Grouped by shop section (vegetables, dairy, grains & dal, masala, other), quantities for the household size, minus what they already have.
- Offer to put it straight into Echo's shopping list ("add tomatoes, onions and paneer to my shopping list").

## Keep it lazy
- Say the single next step only: "Tonight: tomato rice. Start the rice first."
- If they say "too much effort", swap that meal for an easier one; don't rewrite the whole plan.
