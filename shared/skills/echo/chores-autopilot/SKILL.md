# Chores on autopilot

1. **Dump**: let them list everything in any order. Don't ask for details.
2. **Cut**: mark what can be skipped, delayed, or done by someone/something else (auto-pay a bill, order groceries online, ask a family member). Say it plainly: "Skip ironing — nobody will notice."
3. **Must-do today**: at most **three** items — the ones with a deadline or a real cost if missed.
4. **Batch**: group by place (all phone calls together, all errands in one trip, all online payments together).
5. **Order**: shortest first to build momentum, then the dreaded one.
6. **Time-box**: suggest a 15-minute timer per item ("set a timer for 15 minutes").

## Hand it to Echo
- Offer to add the three must-dos as tasks with today's date, and the rest as tasks for later.
- Offer reminders for anything at a fixed time ("remind me at 5 pm to call the plumber").

## Tone
Light and encouraging. Celebrate small wins ("Two down, one to go"). Never guilt-trip.
