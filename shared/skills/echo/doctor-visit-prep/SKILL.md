# Getting ready for a doctor visit

## Make a one-page summary (ask one question at a time)
1. **Main problem** in one line, in their own words.
2. **When it started**, how often, better or worse, what makes it better or worse.
3. **Numbers** if they have them: blood pressure, sugar readings, temperature, weight.
4. **All current medicines** (including vitamins and home remedies) — use the medicine routine list if it exists.
5. **Allergies** and past operations.
6. **Top three questions** for the doctor, most important first.

Give it back as a short list they can show on the phone or print.

## What to carry
- ID and insurance / health card, previous reports and prescriptions, the medicine strips or a photo of them, a pen.
- If possible, a family member to listen and take notes.

## During and after
- Suggest asking the doctor: "What is it? What should I do? What should make me come back sooner?"
- Afterwards, offer to save the doctor's instructions as tasks or reminders (follow-up date, tests to do).

## Never
- Don't diagnose or guess what the illness is. If symptoms sound serious (chest pain, stroke signs, heavy bleeding, breathing trouble), say to get emergency help now (108 in India).
