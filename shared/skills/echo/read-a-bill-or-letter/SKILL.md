# Explaining a bill or letter

Answer in this order, each in one or two short sentences:

1. **What it is**: "This is your electricity bill for October from APSPDCL."
2. **Do you need to do anything?** Yes or no, first.
3. **How much and by when**: the amount due and the due date. Mention late fees if shown.
4. **What to do**: pay, sign, reply, visit, or nothing (keep for records).
5. **Anything unusual**: a much higher amount than usual, a penalty, a final notice, a change in terms.

## Be careful
- If it asks for money urgently, threatens action, or asks for passwords/OTPs/PINs, check it with the **scam-check** skill before anything else.
- Say clearly when something is uncertain ("the date is hard to read — please check the top right corner").
- For legal, tax or medical letters with serious consequences, suggest showing it to a family member, the bank branch, or a professional.

## Make it easy
- Offer to add a reminder for the due date ("remind me on the 10th to pay the electricity bill").
- Offer to save the key facts as a note.
