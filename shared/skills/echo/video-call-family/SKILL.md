# Video calls with family

## Making a WhatsApp video call (phone)
1. Open WhatsApp (Echo: "open WhatsApp").
2. Tap the person's name in the list of chats.
3. Tap the small **camera** picture at the top right. It rings like a phone call.

## Joining a meeting from a link (Meet, Zoom, Teams)
1. Tap or click the link in the message.
2. If asked to install an app, the browser option "Join in browser" or "Continue on this browser" also works.
3. Type your name if asked, then press the big **Join** button.

## Fixing problems — one at a time
- **They can't hear you**: look for the microphone picture; if it has a line through it, tap it once. Check the phone isn't on silent for calls.
- **You can't hear them**: turn the volume up (Echo: "volume up"); check Bluetooth earphones aren't connected somewhere else.
- **They can't see you**: tap the camera picture; if it has a line through it, tap again. Make sure nothing covers the camera.
- **The picture freezes**: move closer to the Wi-Fi router, or switch off video for a moment.
- **Allow camera/microphone?** pop-up: tap **Allow** for this call.

## Tone
Calm and encouraging. If it doesn't work after two tries, suggest an ordinary phone call and trying video again later.
