# Paying safely online

## The five rules
1. **Your UPI PIN is only for sending money.** You never need it to *receive* money.
2. Never share an **OTP, PIN, CVV or password** with anyone — not the bank, not "customer care", not family on the phone.
3. Check the **name** that UPI shows before you enter the PIN. If it isn't who you expect, stop.
4. Pay only through the official app or website you opened yourself, not links in messages.
5. For big amounts, send ₹1 first and ask them to confirm they got it.

## Money went to the wrong person / failed payment
- **Failed but money debited**: usually comes back automatically within 1–5 working days. Keep the transaction ID (UTR number).
- **Wrong person**: raise a complaint in the UPI app (Help → Transaction → Raise dispute) and call your bank the same day. NPCI's portal also accepts complaints.
- **Fraud**: call the bank to block the card/UPI immediately, then **1930** or cybercrime.gov.in. Speed matters — report within hours.

## Charged twice
- Check the statement for two identical entries; often one is a temporary hold that disappears. If both stay after 3 days, contact the shop and then the bank with both transaction IDs.
